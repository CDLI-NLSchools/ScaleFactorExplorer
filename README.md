# Scale Factor Explorer

Math 2201, Unit 6: Proportional Reasoning. Indicators 6.5, 6.6, 6.10, 6.11, 6.13 and 6.14.

A formative exploration app in three modes:

- **Explore k**: rectangles, squares, cubes, rectangular prisms and cylinders with live dimension and k sliders. Lengths, perimeter, area, surface area and volume update beside k, k squared and k cubed readouts, and both figures are drawn to one shared scale.
- **Predict, Then Reveal**: a stated scaling hides the new area, surface area or volume until the student commits a prediction. Feedback names the classic slips (multiplied by k once, wrong power of k, arithmetic slip).
- **Work Backwards**: recover k from an area ratio (square root) or a volume ratio (cube root), then apply k to a second measure, including area-ratio-to-volume and volume-ratio-to-surface-area crossovers. Feedback names the wrong root, the ratio used as k, and the reciprocal.

Every checked task lands on a printable exploration record with predictions beside revealed values.

Single self-contained `index.html`, no external dependencies. `harness.js` independently recomputes the mathematics (54,512 assertions, PASS).
